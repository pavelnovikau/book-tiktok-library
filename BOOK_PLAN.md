### Meditations — direct quotation requirement

Pavel specifically asked not to lose the quotations from Marcus Aurelius. Treat *Meditations / Наедине с собой* as personal philosophical notes addressed to oneself. Preserve the work's voice through exact, source-verified key quotations throughout the nested depths, paired with clearly distinct explanation and application. Do not replace quotations with polished paraphrases or add quotation marks to our own synthesis.

Each quotation needs a stable book/section locator (for example, IV.3), translator and edition/source link. RU and EN must refer to the same passage; verify each against its own named translation rather than translating one quotation ourselves and presenting the result as a published translation. Any omission must be visible and must preserve the sentence's meaning. Maintain a quotation ledger in the collector's existing sources.md and validate quoted strings against the selected primary text. Search-engine quote cards and unsourced attributed aphorisms are not evidence.

Identified RU starting point: Semyon Rogovin's 1914 translation, indexed at https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Наедине_с_собой_(Марк_Аврелий;_Роговин). The page is explicitly unreviewed; check the selected passages against a scan or another reliable witness before quoting them. Select and verify an exact EN edition separately. The original work is ancient, but modern translations must not be treated as automatically public domain.

## Home discovery — approved approach, 2026-10-31

Pavel accepted the universal approach with “ок”: one common catalogue, search, “Все / Художественное / Нон-фикшн” and recognizable genres or subjects. Both he and his son should quickly find books suitable for themselves, and the son's books must not look like a “children's” collection. Remove the public “Сыну · 14+ / For my son · 14+” presentation when implementing the new navigation; keep honest internal audience/context metadata. Implementation is published via PR #23 and independently verified in production at 31 books.

- Latest clarification supersedes the personal-shelf idea: Pavel wants a universal approach, found “личная полка” confusing, and asked for one understandable library. Use one common catalogue for everyone. Do not introduce named-reader profiles or personal recommendations as a prerequisite for choosing a book.
- Keep search prominent. Provide “Все / Художественное / Нон-фикшн” as the broad catalogue entry points. Adult fiction remains discoverable; nonfiction is not an age gate.
- Within fiction, use recognizable genres: “Фантастика”, “Фэнтези”, “Приключения”, “О жизни и людях”. Within nonfiction, use subjects: “Мышление и решения”, “Психология и отношения”, “Философия и смысл”, “Время и привычки”, “Здоровье и тело”, “Системы и общество”, “История и власть”, “Технологии и будущее”. Do not display every filter as a permanent multi-row wall; a compact genre/subject selector can expose the relevant options with result counts.
- A book can match several genres/subjects but appears once in results. Separate work type, genres, themes and author in the data model; the present single section plus mixed collections cannot express all these independently. Taleb and Sapolsky remain searchable authors or optional curated collections rather than top-level genres.
- Recompute genre/subject counts within the current type/query context. Provide an obvious return to all books and a clear-filter action. Do not carry the catalogue-maintenance term “Без подборки / No collection” into the new public discovery controls.
- Search title, author, RU/EN names, localized genres/themes and a small controlled set of useful aliases. Current raw English tags alone do not make Russian topic queries work. Existing local reading progress remains available; this proposal does not require a profile or account system.
- For example, *I, Robot* can match fiction, science fiction and ethics; *The Hobbit* fiction, fantasy and adventure; *Meditations* nonfiction, philosophy and personal practice. Show readable book metadata and a concrete one-sentence reason to choose a book, with the same editorial and visual treatment for all readers.
- Proposed first-screen order: search and “Все / Художественное / Нон-фикшн”, a compact genre/subject trigger, then books. A teenager can choose science fiction and an adult can choose philosophy or the same science fiction. Keep the existing shared icon-only reader header contract unchanged.
