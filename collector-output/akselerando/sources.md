## What supports this version

The complete primary HTML was lawfully obtained from the author's own site. Its title/copyright/publication history and all nine chapter divisions were inspected. **Access to a complete file is not a claim of continuous reading of every line.** This repair read selected continuous scene passages throughout all nine chapters, including their openings, developments and outcomes, with additional sustained reading of the key negotiations, family transitions, escape mechanism, Saturn death/election and final confrontation. The resulting plot arc was checked against those actual passages before authoring. It is a deliberately short compression, not an edition-complete substitute or a certification of every detail in the novel.

No review, teaching summary, glossary or remembered adaptation supplies a missing plot event. All story events refer to source **1**, the primary edition. Old review panels, topical predictions and portable lessons have been removed. The text does not assert a single definitive authorial message.

English and Russian are original summary prose. Russian is not either published translation; the existing title/slug «Акселерандо» / `akselerando` is retained. No dialogue, quotations, whole chapters, publisher cover or translation text is reproduced. Proper names and the original title identify the work. The source edition records CC BY-NC-ND 2.5; that metadata is not represented as permission to distribute an altered full edition. This package contains original concise summary prose and an original typographic cover, not the source book. Raw HTML and extraction remain outside the public repository.

## Narrative and spoiler boundaries

**14 Essential / 26 Core / 52 Deep** cumulative screens. Essential tells the initial Manfred-to-Amber route to the threshold of first contact; Core expands the relationships and events along that same route. Neither promises the rest of the novel without spoilers. Deep alone contains Wunch violence, the passage through the router, Saturn's later events, the election result, Manni's identity and the final Aineko confrontation. Cover, final, source notes and the generic Continue label disclose none of those outcomes. The Deep warning itself is behind mode consent. External primary links contain the full plot.

Aineko's statements about manipulating the family remain **claims by Aineko**, and Manfred's staged-ending explanation remains his **suspicion**. The ghost's immense elapsed-time claim is not asserted as reliable. The consciousness/status of the decoy world's inhabitants is not settled by the digest: Sadeq claims they are not real people; Amber asks. The death of Aineko's avatar is not described as destruction of the whole intelligence. The one-bit test is a proposed bargain, with the ending's uncertainty retained.

## Limits and independent check

This is not a claim to have continuously read the full 146,000-plus-word web text or collated a Russian edition. Review of the revised 52 bilingual pairs and their scene sequence is complete at implementation level; independent review is still required. Selection omits many interludes, subplots, descriptions and conversations. It does not replace them with invented scenes. Source access is complete; the reading coverage is selected and explicitly scoped.
