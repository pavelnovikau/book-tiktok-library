## Specific limits retained

Cristo's inference is qualified; Balthazar's conviction is not a proved biological identity. Guttiere is his foster daughter. Olsen is a factory worker. The operation remains fictional and is explained only as Salvator's account, without clinical claims. The doctor's hope of recovery/refuge is not an accomplished cure or a narrated arrival. The first/second marriage and escape are Deep-only. Neither Zurita's convenient death nor a lovers' reunion has been invented. Numbers/history from the doctor's speech that do not carry the story were omitted, rather than silently corrected into new authorial dialogue.
