## Claim map fixed before screen writing

Question/desire: How can decisions improve without reliably forecasting rare events?
Problem: apparent stability, optimized buffers, forced commitments and hidden nonlinear downside can conceal exposure to shocks. Surviving yesterday does not measure tomorrow's tail.
Thesis: fragile loses, robust preserves, antifragile benefits from specified variability; response shape and survival matter more than the event label.
Mechanism: nonlinear payoff → asymmetric response → bounded failures and retained upside → selection/learning, conditional on affordable trials, useful feedback, exercise rights and no ruin.
Architecture: I triad/overcompensation/layers; II suppression/naive intervention/prediction; III Seneca/nonprediction/barbell; IV optionality/technology/tinkering/knowledge; V nonlinearity/model error; VI subtraction/time/Lindy/medicine; VII ethical allocation of fragility. The sequence preserves this seven-book movement, while inserting explicit mathematical explanations and objections. It does not claim chapter-by-chapter coverage.
Evidence: author explanations and technical derivations establish what Taleb argues; IMF coauthored working paper illustrates a method, not independent validation; CAST tests an intervention's harm; supervised training trials bound biological analogies; WHO bounds resistance and access; Ostrom challenges simple central/local binaries.
Consequences: change exposure before demanding forecasts; fund buffers and reversible exploration; retain learning and gains; measure costs to outsiders.
Objections: finite local curvature is not a global theorem; options have costs and can expire; selection kills units; nature is not an ethical authority; nonintervention also harms; Lindy is conditional survival, not truth; political analogies need evidence.
Durable model: specify object, stressor, payoff, range, horizon and loss bearer; protect continuation, then seek affordable asymmetric upside.

### Transparent survival derivation

Let T be a nonnegative lifetime. For a threshold t > 0 with P(T > t) > 0, assume an **exact, untruncated** survival tail S(u) = P(T > u) = C·u^(−α) for every u ≥ t, α > 1. Then

`m(t) = E[T − t | T > t] = ∫₀∞ P(T > t+x | T > t) dx = ∫ₜ∞ S(u)/S(t) du = t^α ∫ₜ∞ u^(−α) du = t/(α−1)`.

This identity follows by integrating the conditional nonnegative lifetime. NIST supplies the residual-life definition and generalized-Pareto context, not evidence for applying this distribution to books or technologies. If α ≤ 1 the mean diverges; if the power law is only approximate or truncated, the exact formula need not hold. Conditional expected **total** life is t + m(t), distinct from remaining life. The screen states the assumption and calculation in both languages.
