It does not claim to replace every chapter or certify complete-book/authorized-translation fidelity.

The public author pages are excerpts selected and sometimes adapted by Clear, not a complete 2018 edition. Modern scientific efficacy of every underlying study was not separately established; examples are attributed to Clear. No numerical guarantee or new health procedure is made.

Physical iPhone/PWA behavior, touch navigation and deployed integration remain untested by this worker.

No quotations or page references have been fabricated. All reading prose is original paraphrase. No private copyrighted full text or extraction is in this collector. No purchase, account change, loan, credential request, unofficial full-book download or access bypass was used.
