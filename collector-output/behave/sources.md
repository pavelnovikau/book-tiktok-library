## Claim map established before screens

Question / desire: understand how one species can protect and destroy, without mistaking a label for a cause.
Problem: assigning an act to a person, hormone, region or gene cuts the causal chain too early.
Thesis: explain a contextualized act through interacting histories on nested timescales.
Mechanism: act → preceding neural state → sensory interpretation → recent endocrine state → plasticity → adolescence → early development → genetic regulation → cultural/ecological history → evolution. Then return to Us/Them, hierarchy, morality, empathy, dehumanization, justice, war/peace and change.
Evidence: separate animal mechanisms, human observations, randomized tasks/interventions, mathematical models and historical documents. Do not use one as a substitute for another.
Consequence: ask where a change in context could alter later probabilities; explanation alone neither excuses harm nor establishes a policy.
Objections: reverse inference; replication; candidate-gene failures; task/population specificity; uncertain causal transfer; author’s determinism is a philosophical interpretation, not a scan result.
Durable model: causes are layered and context-sensitive; plasticity is also biological. Reject single-factor biological fatalism without misrepresenting Sapolsky as endorsing libertarian free will.
Gaps: full EN/RU text unavailable; exhaustive chapter-note verification, all book anecdotes, Russian translation wording and page citations unavailable. Chapter mapping below is an editorial alignment to exposed contents, not a claim that every screen appears in the book.

## Scientific update register

- Brain-wide individual-difference associations: Marek 2022; does not imply every small within-person neuroscience experiment is invalid.
- Ego depletion: Hagger 2016 is already pre-publication; included as an epistemic caution, not mislabeled a later discovery.
- Testosterone: 2020 meta-analysis; average associations weak, experimental estimate includes zero.
- Oxytocin: 2023 vole knockout challenges necessity claims; no human treatment inference or claim the molecule is irrelevant.
- Adolescence: 2023 multi-dataset trajectory and 2024 review oppose a universal birthday of brain maturity.
- Neurogenesis: 2026 post-mortem molecular evidence; no inference that adult humans can grow morality through exercises.
- Genetics: Border 2019 rejects tested candidate-gene depression hypotheses, not all G×E; 2024 score review and 2026 cross-population study retain context/portability cautions.
- Marshmallow: Watts 2018 adjusts associations; does not prove self-control irrelevant.
- Disgust: Landy 2015 is pre-publication; caution about provocative causal claims.
- Contact: Paluck et al. 2019 narrows causal/transfer confidence relative to broad 2006 evidence.

## Access limitations

Publisher and PubMed retrieval intermittently returned redirects, CAPTCHA challenges or empty extraction. Where this occurred, only the indexed abstract / author-repository summary actually exposed was used, as identified per source. Neither access controls nor membership gates were bypassed. Scientific source consultation ranges from indexed abstract to selected open results, not full systematic review of every paper.
