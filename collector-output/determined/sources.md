# Determined — source ledger and claim map

Access date: 2026-10-31. Analytical synthesis of Robert M. Sapolsky, Determined: A Science of Life Without Free Will (Penguin Press, 2023). Official UK preview uses The Science… in its subtitle. No full lawful local edition has been confirmed; the entire book was not read. No unauthorized editions were sought. No publisher artwork, copied book passages, invented quotations or book page locators are used.

## Claim map established before screen writing

Desire: understand choice without either cruelty or resignation. Problem: the felt moment of deciding hides how the deciding person was formed. Author thesis: intentions and self-control arise from intertwined biological/environmental histories; he rejects ultimate self-authorship and basic-desert praise/blame. Mechanism: seconds of neural activity, current state, learning, development, prenatal conditions, genes, culture and evolution are continuous interacting explanations, not independent percentage shares. Book architecture: chapters 1–4 causation/intent/grit; 5–6 chaos; 7–8 emergence; 9–10 quantum indeterminacy; 10.5 interlude; 11 social consequences; 12 change; 13 prior shifts in blame; 14 punishment; 15 poverty.

Empirical scaffolding: Libet is bounded by timing/method; Schurger and Maoz challenge overgeneralization; marshmallow and grit findings are qualified and include conflicting reanalysis; population neuroscience and prenatal research cannot predict an individual. Consequences: author challenges retribution and moralized merit, while a digest synthesis separates causal explanation, legal judgment, public protection and policy evaluation. Objections: compatibilist reasons-responsiveness and ownership, historical manipulation, libertarian event/agent causation, luck, underdetermination of metaphysics by science. Durable model: trace the history, identify present capacities, reduce harm, evaluate interventions, retain uncertainty.
