# Ender’s Game — sources and reading scope

2026-10-31. Reviewer working document; linked materials and the evidence map contain ending spoilers. The standalone Sources dialog uses spoiler-safe descriptions.

Work: Orson Scott Card’s 1985 novel. This package does not substitute the 1977 novelette, film or sequel events. It is an original RU/EN condensation, not the novel text or a translation of a published digest.

A scoped exact-title/author metadata search checked 218 OPF candidates in the supplied Apple Books Documents root, expanded and ZIP EPUBs. No exact owned edition matched. No target OPF, spine or body extent was therefore available for inspection. No private/full novel text or unrelated filenames are retained here. No complete novel was read continuously.

The directly consulted opening is the permission-labelled Tor Teen 2013 excerpt (ISBN 9780765337320; movie tie-in packaging, novel text). The actual novel quotations reproduced in Susanne Lindberg’s Halmstad thesis cite Starscape 2002; its bibliography identifies that edition. Kessel’s quoted page references remain locators in his essay, without an invented edition conversion. The detailed Hatrack 2004 and named GradeSaver 2007 guides precede later sequel retellings. The retained coda follows the novel account in those sources.

Retained links are supported individually by selected primary passages, actual author/publisher material and accountable chapter accounts. Teaching questions are not answers, a publisher blurb does not certify later scenes, and criticism is not treated as the novel’s own verdict. Consulted source roles are listed below. Exact primary hinges and limitations are in evidence/primary-hinges.md; every narrative screen is mapped in evidence/screen-map.json. No whole-book fidelity or independent review certificate is claimed.

## Rights and omissions

All reader prose is original paraphrase. No invented quotations, page references, scenes, dialogue, examples, exercises or management lessons are included. No private copyrighted source was copied into the package. No claim is made to continuous reading from having accessed a page or excerpt.

The condensation omits detailed family religion, most minor cadets, some intermediate army transfers and battles, exact ship counts/odds/weapon radii, extensive human geopolitics and later-series stories. These are omissions, not gaps silently filled from memory. The particular retained story has no identified essential missing action or causal link after the targeted checks; an independent reviewer can still challenge support for each link.

Source 4 is a substantial fifteen-chapter teaching guide hosted by the author, not a text written by Card in every section. Only its explicit observations/anchor quotations and scene locations are used. Source 3 is an official publisher guide with prompts and selected quotations, not a full novel or an answer key. Source 5 is Card’s own commentary. Sources 6–7 are critical wrappers containing selected primary quotations; their interpretations are not put into the narrative as settled authorial intent. Sources 8–24 are acknowledged guide-backed evidence, with specific embedded author passages separately identified in the hinge map.
