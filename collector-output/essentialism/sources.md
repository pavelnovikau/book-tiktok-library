## Claim map — established before screen drafting

1. **Desire / central question:** make a meaningful contribution without permanently scattering limited attention. Which commitments deserve scarce time and energy?
2. **Problem:** automatic agreement, diffuse criteria and social pressure accumulate commitments. Success itself attracts options, undermining the focus that enabled it (ch.1).
3. **Thesis:** systematically distinguish the few contributions that matter, eliminate competing commitments and design execution with less friction. Doing less is a means; better contribution is the criterion.
4. **Foundations:** exercise choice (ch.2); acknowledge unequal returns (ch.3 plus continuation files); accept trade-offs rather than promising everything (ch.4). Do not recast this as unlimited personal control or a requirement to abandon health/family.
5. **Explore:** protect thinking space (5); look for meaning, not merely facts (6); play (7); protect sleep/the human asset (8); set explicit, selective criteria (9). Explore more possibilities than one commits to. The 90% heuristic rejects middling options; the book explicitly admits a better opportunity may not arrive. Incoming options: all three minimum criteria and at least two of three ideal criteria.
6. **Eliminate:** define concrete, inspiring essential intent (10); separate refusal of a request from rejection of a person and negotiate displacement with a boss (11); reconsider sunk costs, ownership, status quo and use reverse pilots (12); edit by cutting, condensing, correcting and exercising restraint (13); establish boundaries and social contracts in advance (14). Help is not prohibited; avoid silently taking over others' responsibility.
7. **Execute:** reserve buffers and prepare early (15); find and remove the binding obstacle (16); make small meaningful progress and preparations visible (17); use routines and cues (18); focus attention on what matters now without abandoning planning (19).
8. **Transformation:** repeated practice and identity rather than one-off cleanup (20). Leadership appendix makes clarity, role ownership, selective hiring, communication and supportive accountability organizational responsibilities.
9. **Evidence:** Trisha Morse, author/family story, Southwest/Continental, journalism lesson, selection examples, unfinished digital-access intent, Herbie (explicitly a fictional example from *The Goal*), Phelps. These illustrate, not estimate a treatment effect.
10. **Independent checks:** sleep restriction has laboratory support for cumulative performance impairment (source 4); subtraction can be overlooked in controlled tasks (source 5, postdates the book). Neither establishes the whole book, the 90% cutoff, or a universal 50% buffer.
11. **Counterarguments / limits:** autonomy and resources differ; duties of care cannot be scored as optional invitations; subjective criteria can become arbitrary; exploration needs a stopping condition; strict cutoffs risk premature rejection; calm routines need review; buffers cost capacity; elite examples have selection bias. These are digest synthesis, not smuggled in as author admissions.
12. **Durable model:** choose deliberately, investigate before committing, remove to protect the chosen contribution, build reliability around it, review when conditions change. Essential must contain thesis, cycle, key tools, organizational dimension, constraints and final model by itself.
