The strongest primary hinges here are **quoted novel passages within those guides**: Charlie’s reason for volunteering (Report 6), food contingent on a maze (Report 8), Nemur’s language about making Charlie a person and Burt’s response (Report 13), Charlie’s fear at his father’s shop (Report 14), the research formulation (Report 16), his refusal to discard the former Charlie’s life (Report 17), and the final request as quoted in SparkNotes Report 17. The author’s interview corroborates his refusal of an imposed happy ending; it does not verify every late scene.

This is a bounded sufficiency judgement for the retained approximately 2,000 English words: substantial accountable event guides plus checked quoted primary hinges, with original author material. It is not a claim of full-novel fidelity or translation collation. An independent checker must assess this boundary, rather than treating the structural audit or two agreeing recaps as blanket certification. ## Editorial decisions and corrections made during implementation

- `bakery-dismissal`: removed the draft’s “They sign a petition” / “Они собирают подписи”. The consulted Report 11 cross-check establishes coworker pressure, not the retained package’s claimed signature detail. This is an unsupported-specificity removal, not a declaration that a petition never appears in any edition.
- `gimpys-choice`: replaced the draft’s generic hostility with reluctant agreement, supported by [SparkNotes Reports 10–11](https://www.sparknotes.com/lit/algernon/section3/).
- `alice-stays` / `unreadable-paper`: fixed a compressed transition that placed Alice’s departure before Charlie failed to understand his own paper. The final two-screen sequence follows [SparkNotes Report 17](https://www.sparknotes.com/lit/algernon/section8/): intimacy and promise, inability to understand paper, conflict and her departure, later efforts to learn.
- `fathers-barbershop`: explicitly kept a feared rejection as Charlie’s anticipation. The quoted Report 14 passage does not establish what Matt would actually have done.
- `chicago-presentation`: did not convert Charlie’s imagined showman introduction into an actual speech.
- Excluded the guide’s conjectured childhood electroshock explanation, any invented surgery mechanism and the interpretation that the book proves intelligence itself destroys kindness.
- No direct dialogue, imitation of the printed progress reports, invented medical explanation or reader coaching is present. The research relation stays inside the novel’s experiment.
