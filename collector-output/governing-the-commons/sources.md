## Sufficiency decision for this particular retained arc

The combination supports a concise argument: distinguish CPRs from public goods and property regimes; explain temptation and changeable rules; show actual self-governance mechanisms; distinguish endurance from origins; describe conditional principles; show institutional mixtures and failure; preserve scale and performance qualifications. Each retained procedure has an author-text locator, and each case's connection to the target work is checked against Cambridge material. The principles are tied to 1990 by the authors' explicit note, rather than guessed from the Nobel lecture.

This is a **selective source-supported condensation**, not a substitute for reading or exhaustively checking the complete edition. The full Spanish huerta and Philippine zanjera procedures, all basin histories, the complete cross-case failure comparison, detailed mathematical analysis and every causal qualification in chapters 2–6 have not been reconstructed. These omissions limit completeness; no retained route requires those unverified details. No claim is made about the unknown founders' motives, fictional dialogue, universal community success, or current conditions in the historical places.

No unofficial complete-book mirror was opened or downloaded. No purchase, loan, credentials, subscription or access permissions were changed. Official excerpts/author files were held only in an owned external temporary directory for consultation and removed after verification. Only original prose, URLs, locators, metadata and verification evidence remain in this collector package.
