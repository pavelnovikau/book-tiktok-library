## Work and editions

Robert A. Heinlein, *Have Space Suit—Will Travel*, the **1958 prose novel**, first published by Charles Scribner’s Sons. This package concerns Kip Russell, Peewee, and the Mother Thing. It does not use the later graphic adaptation, a film proposal, another Heinlein juvenile, or an unrelated novel called *Outward Bound*.

The publicly accessible author-text witness is the **2005 electronic sample hosted by Baen**, ISBN 9781416505495. Its copyright/edition notice explicitly identifies the 1958 first printing and a Baen electronic version. Its contents link to **Tony Daniel’s introduction and chapters 1–3**. The introduction is commentary, not a fourth Heinlein chapter. The text is an excerpt of the actual novel, not a modern condensed summary; it is not a complete edition. No complete novel was fetched. Selected passages of these three chapters were read and rechecked; this is not a claim of continuous reading even of every paragraph in the sample.

The later story is supported by **Robert James, Ph.D., *Have Spacesuit, Will Travel Lesson Plans***, linked from the official Virginia Edition publisher’s resource page. This is a **26-page accountable educational guide**, keyed to a **Del Rey / Ballantine paperback**; its exact printing/ISBN is not stated. Its chapter summaries on PDF pages **2–17** cover all twelve chapters in sequence and contain short passages from Heinlein. Those summaries were read; selected questions on pp.17–19 were also consulted. The rest of its teaching apparatus was not read as if it were the novel. The guide’s literary, political, educational and gender interpretations are James’s, and have not been imported into this narrative as the author’s message.

The Russian copy is a new original retelling aligned with the English condensation, **not** an excerpt from, reconstruction of, or certified translation of a published Russian edition. Naming choices are Кип Рассел / Kip Russell, Пьюи / Peewee (Patricia Reisfeld), Мать / the Mother Thing, Оскар / Oscar, Червемордый / Wormface, станция Томбо / Tombaugh Station, Юний / Iunio. These are local rendering choices, not a claim to reproduce one translator’s spellings.

## Discovery limits and exclusions

- The World Space Week English teacher kit was discoverable in search, but its direct PDF URL returned **404**. Cached excerpts led to further source checks; they are **not** part of the final evidence set. A tempting shoes/lunar fall detail was removed rather than relying on that unavailable witness. Its erroneous mixture of a 1958 date and Ballantine attribution is not adopted as an edition record.
- The Simon & Schuster URL returned **403** to the direct request. No excerpt from it is claimed read. No access controls were bypassed.
- Tony Daniel’s Baen introduction was identified as a different author’s essay and excluded as a source of Heinlein scenes.
- The publisher’s digital graphic novel was identified and excluded. No purchases, loans, account changes, credentials, unofficial full-text hunting, or external messages were used.
- No complete-primary-text fidelity or continuous full-book reading is certified. The support is adequate for the **retained compact arc at the granularity below**, while fine dialogue, precise staging of unconsulted scenes, exact later chapter wording, omissions, and edition variation remain unverified. These limits do not license additions from memory.
