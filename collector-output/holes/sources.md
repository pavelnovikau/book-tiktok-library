## Work and edition

Louis Sachar, **Holes**, first published in August 1998 by Frances Foster Books / Farrar, Straus and Giroux, New York. The original novel is the target, not the film, *Small Steps*, the survival-guide companion, a simplified adaptation or an existing Russian translation. Russian names and prose here are this condensation's own rendering.

Primary edition actually consulted: **Bloomsbury YA, 2023**, publisher sample of ePDF **9781526634191**, also identifying paperback **9781408865231** and EPUB **9781408810279**. Copyright page states original text © Louis Sachar 1998 and distinguishes the 2023 additional material. This is a **28-PDF-page sample**, not a full edition: the narrative consulted runs through **chapters 1–4 and the opening of chapter 5**, printed pp. 3–16 (PDF pp. 15–28). The first four chapters were read through; chapter 5 cuts off mid-sentence. No OPF/spine was available for this PDF, and none is invented. Its publisher watermark/metadata and body extent establish it as a sample of the novel, not the novel's complete text.

The full 1998 novel was **not obtained or read continuously**. Later actions are supported by the substantial Scholastic guide, its embedded primary passages, exact publisher quotations, the author's own Q&A and the specifically read SparkNotes chapter accounts. The map distinguishes direct primary reading, short primary quotations embedded in a guide and secondary plot support. Their chapter coverage is never relabeled as reading those full novel chapters.
