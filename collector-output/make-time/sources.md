## Coherent claim map

Desire: have a memorable day containing something personally important, rather than simply finish more incoming work.
Problem: the Busy Bandwagon makes busyness respectable; Infinity Pools make distraction frictionless and inexhaustible. Efficiency fills its own freed space with new demands, while willpower repeatedly faces systems optimized for engagement.
Thesis: redesign daily defaults to make room for one chosen Highlight. Protect attention with barriers, support attention through bodily energy, and adjust the arrangement using brief reflection.
Causal chain: choose a meaningful middle-sized activity → reserve actual calendar space and accept a trade-off → add friction to competing stimuli and ease the entry into the chosen activity → maintain enough energy to choose rather than react → record outcomes → retain, modify, or discard tactics → repeat. Energy and attention interact; this is a loop rather than four rigid time blocks.
Evidence: authors’ product-design experience (Gmail/YouTube), design-sprint observations, self-experiments, readers’ stories, and selected scientific references. These are different evidence classes. A successful sprint does not validate the whole daily system, and personal notes cannot identify a universal causal effect.
Consequences: satisfaction and agency may precede higher output; family, leisure, craft and obligations can all be valid Highlights. Repeated experience may reveal a different life direction, but quitting a job or sailing away is not the prescribed outcome.
Qualifications: only one Highlight, not only one task; 60–90 minutes is an author heuristic; defaults change with circumstances; morning/evening and fasting/snacking disagreements are preserved; tactics are 87 candidate experiments, not 87 obligations. Calendar control, caregiving, work roles, health, budget and shared-household needs constrain choice.
Objection: a toolbox can feel like familiar tips and transfer planning costs to the reader. The four-step loop gives the tips a selection criterion, but does not establish that everyone can gain the same freedom.
Durable model: choose → protect → replenish → learn. Measure whether the important thing actually received attention, not the number of tasks completed.

### 6 — Caffeine and sleep (role: primary; independent narrowing)
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3805807/
Alternative direct abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24235903/
Drake, Roehrs, Shambroom & Roth, J Clin Sleep Med (2013), doi:10.5664/jcsm.3170. Randomized crossover study, 12 healthy normal sleepers, fixed 400 mg dose at 0/3/6 hours before bed versus placebo; significant sleep disruption, including at 6 hours. Supports taking late caffeine costs seriously. Does not determine each reader’s cutoff, validate coffee naps or the book’s morning-cortisol delay rule, or imply every beverage/dose has the same result. Coverage: independent sleep trade-off screen.

### 7 — Walking and creative thought (role: primary; independent narrowing)
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24749966/
Oppezzo & Schwartz, J Exp Psychol Learn Mem Cogn (2014), doi:10.1037/a0036577. Four experiments compare seated/walking tasks. Abstract retrieved in search; direct open later yielded only the site security shell. Supports a bounded distinction: divergent idea generation improved, not a universal improvement across all thinking. No extrapolation to daily productivity or validation of the full framework. Coverage: independent walking screen.

### 8 — Independent review (role: review; practical objection)
URL: https://www.blackad.co.uk/make-time-review/
Alan Black, Blackad bookshelf. Positive account of accessible tactics; objections to extreme Wi-Fi restriction. Useful independent reader response, not scientific replication or representative survey. Coverage: household/feasibility trade-off screen.

## Research boundaries

No external source establishes efficacy of the entire loop as a package. Nutrition, fasting, meditation and evolutionary explanations are reported as book claims or explicitly limited synthesis. No clinical protocol, diet prescription, historical reconstruction or contemporary platform audit is offered. The artifact uses original analytical paraphrases and no direct book quotations; names of steps and tactics are identifiers. All non-cover screens attach source IDs; synthesis labels identify the digest’s own inferences.
