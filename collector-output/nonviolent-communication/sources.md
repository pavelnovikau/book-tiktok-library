The third-edition framework is identified by the publisher’s explicit third-edition chapter-2 sample (©2019 on the title leaf and running footer) and the official chapter-14 NVC3 excerpt. No relevant full edition or Smart Reading condensation was found. This does not certify the absence of an unindexed item. No unrelated titles were printed, and no private full text was extracted.

Official CNVC prose is marked as a cross-check written by Myra Walden.

## Remaining limits and deliberately omitted links

- This is a concise account of the central method with named book cases, not an exhaustive summary of every chapter. No continuous cover-to-cover full-book reading is claimed.
- Main-book chapters 4–8 were not read as continuous complete chapters. Core concepts from those chapters have direct primary support in the chapter-1 process, chapter-2 demand definition, chapter-3 Phil exchange, author handout and the clearly identified supplementary author explanations. No unverified chapter-specific case from those missing chapters is added.
- The third edition’s full conflict-resolution/mediation chapter and later counseling chapter are not reconstructed. The missing evidence would be their complete step sequence, qualifications and case transitions; this reader makes no claim to give that full procedure.
- The main-book protective-force chapter was not directly read. Only the principle directly present in the official author quotes and checked in CNVC is retained; its school case, concrete force procedures and detailed institutional claims are absent.
- RU and EN are newly written accounts of the consulted English material. No official Russian translation, translation equivalence between editions, or publisher authorization of this digest is claimed.
- No promise of healing, nervous-system regulation, improved medical outcomes or guaranteed resolution is invented. Anger/stimulus/need language is attributed to Rosenberg’s model rather than presented as an independently established universal clinical theory.

These are explicit scope boundaries, not an unsupported verdict that no book can be summarized without a full EPUB. No essential retained causal or procedural link is filled from memory. Independent editorial review must assess these passages and this deliberately selected scope.
