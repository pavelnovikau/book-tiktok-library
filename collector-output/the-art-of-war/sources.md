The full thirteen chapters were read continuously for this repair, including the fire and spy chapters. This is a concise original RU/EN account of that translation; it is not a fresh translation from Chinese or a critical edition. Traditional authorship/dating are not established by the site's metadata. The owner's Russian edition was identified but not used as the primary reading base. Later commentaries and contemporary scholarship were removed from the reading sequence, along with modern management applications. Old source-ledger claims are historical, retained in the external baseline rather than silently inherited.

All screen prose is newly written. No quotations or publisher illustrations. No full text or private extraction is redistributed. The public-domain text's figures and confident predictions belong to the historical argument and are not certified empirical laws. The book's deception, conquest, coercion, discipline and instrumental treatment of people are preserved in context.
