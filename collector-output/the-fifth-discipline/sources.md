## Argument map and distinctions retained

- Learning changes capacity to create; adaptation is necessary and does not exhaust generative learning. Training/information alone is insufficient.
- Job boundaries, remote effects, delayed consequences and defensive appearances obstruct learning even among capable people. The three-bolt story remains an attributed Senge account.
- Beer-game shortage, repeated ordering and later surplus explain endogenous amplification. The pipeline is not erased when current inventory is low. Senge’s simplified account has retailer/wholesaler/brewery; Sterman’s standard board adds a distributor. No four-link timing/cost parameters were imported into Senge’s version.
- Reinforcing versus balancing feedback, delays, dynamic versus detail complexity, limits to growth, shifting the burden, eroding goals and growth/underinvestment retain their causal mechanisms. The author’s construction, personnel-staff and sales-discount examples remain author examples, not worker-created cases.
- Personal mastery is proficiency/continuing development, not domination. Creative tension keeps vision and reality together; the gap can be closed by improving reality or lowering vision. Fritz’s contribution is retained.
- Mental models are tacit assumptions open to testing. Inquiry accompanies advocacy. Shell’s scenario planning remains Senge’s causal account, not an independently established sole explanation of corporate success.
- Shared vision is genuinely shared aspiration, distinct from compliance with a leader’s vision. Team dialogue holds assumptions open to examination; apparent harmony and individual intelligence do not establish team learning.
- Systems thinking integrates the other four and also needs them. The 2006 revised introduction’s three capabilities and the ongoing, unfinished nature of practice remain visible.
- Leadership as designer/teacher/steward and limits of simulations are anchored to the author article; the 2006 widened social/ecological frame is anchored to the actual revised introduction.

## Limits and deliberately excluded material

No reconstruction of every later case, every law, every archetype or the full new Part IV. People Express’s full rise/fall, later practitioner narratives and the coda are omitted because their full argument was not checked in the retained edition. The boiled-frog parable, team-IQ number and broad biological/scientific claims are not repeated as verified facts. No invented team/project analogies, dialogue, exercises, page-specific book quotations, numerical effectiveness guarantees or outside prescriptions were added.

Public deliverables contain original condensed prose, bibliographic metadata and links only. Publisher PDFs, OCR and source-page images remain outside the repository. They were not copied into the collector package.
