## Particular missing chain

A faithful practical condensation needs to show **how competing motives produce persistent muscular/postural organization; how the author distinguishes spontaneous action from compulsion; and how his actual readjustment/learning sequence changes that organization**. The publisher blurb and one Guild-cited sentence establish the theme/aim but not this causal and procedural transition. Neither a modern generic Feldenkrais standards document nor another book supplies the missing steps.

Most useful narrowly scoped evidence to close the gap: authorized pages for *Spontaneity and Compulsive Action*, *Motivation and Action / Resistance and Cross Motivation*, the passage linking faulty posture to inhibited action, and the actual *Aim of Readjustment / Correct Posture / First General Overhaul* lesson sequence, with its conditions and stopping cautions.
