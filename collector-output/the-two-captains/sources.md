No invented dialogue or page references. Only the independently verified, public-domain Tennyson motto is quoted briefly.

## Claim / plot / theme map — established before screen writing

1. Desire/question: can a child whose testimony cannot be heard become someone capable of making an ignored truth matter? The central reading lens is acquired agency through language, learning, relationships and evidence.
2. Initial conditions: riverbank letters; a child’s inability to communicate; an accusation against his father; household poverty; a first doctor encounter. Direct sample anchors permit close reading of witnessing, material evidence, adult misunderstanding, child/adult narrative distance and nonheroic care.
3. Development: Moscow school, teacher Korablev, friendship and reading; Katya Tatarinova and her family; a vanished polar expedition; aviation as learned work. These are corroborated through criticism/RSLA rather than a complete primary edition.
4. Investigation mechanism: remembered words acquire context; documents must be compared and interpreted; a convincing story and an established fact are different. The digest draws its own bounded epistemic analogies and does not attribute a modern research checklist to Kaverin.
5. Narrative mechanism: retrospective first-person Sanya, two Katya-narrated parts, documents inside the narrative, expanding geography, coincidence, doubles, genre mixture. Scholarly interpretations are identified as interpretations, not author declarations.
6. Stakes: reputation, trust, love, professional competence, history and public memory; institutions can enable a person while particular authorities misuse power. No implication that disability or poverty is overcome by effort alone.
7. Context: publication in stages (1938–1940 first-book serialization / 1940 first book / 1944 second-book magazine publication / 1945 first complete edition per Vanyukov); 1946 prize. Avoid conflating 1940 first book with the complete two-book novel.
8. Counter-readings: heroic determination can become rigidity; decisive narrative closure is not a guarantee of closure in historical research; a Soviet adventure can be emotionally humane and ideologically selective simultaneously; support does not remove Katya’s agency.
9. Deep-only: an explicitly marked, compact account of the late accusation, wartime betrayal, documentary resolution, reunion and memorial closure. No chapter-by-chapter reconstruction, tactical route, graphic death detail or invented micro-scene.
10. Durable model: care enough to investigate; learn enough to test; distinguish the testimony from the interpretation; remain answerable to people affected by one’s certainty. This is the companion’s synthesis, not a direct Kaverin quotation.

## Evidence exclusions and controls

- No pirate full-text sites, unauthorized downloads, adaptations as substitutes for the novel, fabricated author interviews, exact flight performance figures or clinical diagnoses.
- Search-result snippets alone are discovery, not final evidence. Inaccessible curator interview, Poetry Foundation and unsuccessful unrelated PDF endpoints are not counted as sources. The later successful Geymbukh PDF fetch is recorded above.
- No claim that the fictional ship, captain and culpability simply reproduce a historical expedition. No current scientific/history completion claim from an old search report.
- Film-specific staging is excluded. No external source needs to load for the HTML to work.
- Distinct deeper jobs: mechanisms of testimony, evidence handling, narrative perspective, institution, professional practice, historical convention, counter-reading and restrained ending interpretation. Modern exercises are explicitly synthesis, not new scenes.
