## Boundaries

The systems are explanatory fictions, not anatomical modules; fast thinking is not uniformly bad, slow thinking is not guaranteed correct. Linda is explicitly the authors’ fictional experimental prompt, not an invented event in this condensation. Prospect theory’s limitations, conditions for expert intuition and restrictions on broad risk policies remain visible. Research results are attributed to the book, without asserting that all 2011 claims are settled science in 2026. No purchases, loans, authentication changes or access bypass were used.
