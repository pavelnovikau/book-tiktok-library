## Reading experience

There are 36 Essential screens, 90 Core and 144 Deep. The inventory contains 102 screens labeled synthesis, 30 book, 11 external context and one cover. These numbers alone do not establish a defect; the text does. The first school crisis does not occur until screen 43, after long sequences about recognition, educational fit, restraint, trust and precision. ## Reproduced spoiler leak and local repair

```js
let spoilerConsent = 1;
// A remembered depth does not authorize spoilers in a new reading session.
```
