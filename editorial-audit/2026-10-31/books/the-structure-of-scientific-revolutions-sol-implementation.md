## Delivered reading

The book's explanatory argument is told in original RU/EN prose, using Kuhn's historical cases. Normal science begins with shared achievements and examples, concentrates effort and makes precise expectations possible. Violations can become anomalies, but a discrepancy is not an automatic refutation. Some failures become crises; crisis has three possible endings. A revolution reconstructs conceptual relations and standards as well as answers. Incommensurability complicates comparison without abolishing reasons, communication or progress.

Priestley and Lavoisier remain in Essential, so the shortest route contains an actual historical episode rather than a sequence of definitions. Core adds early optics, learning through exemplars, Cavendish, Ptolemaic repairs, Clairaut's lunar calculation, Newton/Einstein, Galileo's pendulum, values, translation and the evolutionary comparison. Deep adds the matrix/exemplar distinction, Lavoisier's remaining acidity/caloric commitments, X-rays, Bruner/Postman, the postscript's relaxed crisis requirement, explanatory losses, limits of the gestalt analogy and community scale. The closing question is Kuhn's unresolved question, not a new lesson for the reader.

## Route continuity

Essential moves from the historical problem to paradigm, normal work and anomaly; the two oxygen screens make conceptual recognition concrete. It then distinguishes crisis from mere difficulty, supplies alternative endings, explains reconstruction and incommensurability, and closes with problem-solving progress and its limits. It does not promise that all crises produce revolutions or that every view is equally good.

Core preserves that order. The Ptolemaic case prepares crisis; Clairaut prevents interpreting persistence as necessarily blind resistance. Newton/Einstein and the pendulum give different kinds of conceptual change. Shared values and translation explain how partial incommensurability still permits reasoned comparison. The evolutionary comparison prepares the final ontology qualification.

Deep inserts qualifications beside the claims they limit: the imperfect state of oxygen recognition follows Lavoisier; instrumental and perceptual cases follow discovery; the relaxed crisis requirement follows the three outcomes; explanatory losses and gestalt limits sit beside conceptual/world-change passages. The final community-scale clarification returns to the scope of the whole argument without adding a management application.
