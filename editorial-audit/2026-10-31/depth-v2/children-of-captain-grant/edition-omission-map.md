# Edition and omission map

Full-story spoilers. Main retained arc follows the Horne-edited 66-chapter witness. The original French work has 70 chapters; only four selective French checks are claimed. This table does not certify what the uncollated French-only material contains.

The final volume follows developed scenes, not a quota: the first part gives the rescue party time to form, lose Robert, live with Thalcave, lose its false trail and cross the flood; the second develops sea travel, farm trust, the wagon journey, Toline, music, horse losses, betrayal and the stolen order; the third retains the long wrong-ship escape, war/captivity, two rescues, the order’s cause, Ayrton’s limited knowledge, the children’s night and the full message/return. Rough planning range was 12–18k RU narrative words. Final exact counts are in metrics.json. It is a large condensation, not a substitute for every travel chapter or every argument in the original.
