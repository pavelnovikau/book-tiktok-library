# Chapter and scene map

Working review document: contains full-story spoilers. Every narrative paragraph was written in the connected RU draft, translated as a matching EN paragraph, then assigned a scene ID. Horne locators identify parts/chapters and 1-based physical lines in the exact hashed witness, never printed pages.

Essential: cover + narrative paragraphs 1–24 + final (26 screens). A continuous opening ending after Robert’s rescue, the Portuguese misunderstanding and the introduction of Thalcave/Thaouka. Captain Grant is still missing.

Core: cover + paragraphs 1–67 + final (69 screens). The continuous opening through the South American journey and the Australian journey to the Snowy camp, before exposure. It stops honestly with an unresolved search.

Deep: cover + all 113 narrative paragraphs + an inline late-story warning before paragraph 68 + final (116 screens). The three-part journey, betrayal, New Zealand, wrong order, real reunion/message solution, Ayrton disposition and homecoming are retained. Entry to Deep always requires session consent, including saved IDs.
