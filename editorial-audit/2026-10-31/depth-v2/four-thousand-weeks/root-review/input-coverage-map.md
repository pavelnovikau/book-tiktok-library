## Route scope

Essential is the coherent finite-choice argument: email feedback; sacrifice; good procrastination/WIP; commitment and leaving; attention/internal escape; planning uncertainty; present-value/rest; three patience principles and bus; network value of time; ordinary meaning; Jung and action without guaranteed rescue. It does not promise the five-question sequence or the appendix.

Core adds the author’s opening, Cowan/Delhi, existential overload, qualified Heidegger, Abel and priority refusals, perfection legend, watermelon/platforms, responsible planning, baby/aurora, Sabbath/Setiya, reading’s duration/Roberts, desynchronized week and collective participation, all five questions and all ten techniques.

Deep includes all86 body paragraphs, further historical and philosophical reasoning and the Keesmaat/Coutts/Cain/Kafka/Young/Brown/Peck/Hartig/Magee cases. Every chapter’s central chain is represented, with the omissions above. No word count alone establishes adequacy.

The WIP-three, closed-list-ten and one-large-project techniques are distinguished. Patience is not obedience to abuse; commitment does not erase deliberate rejection. Shared time adds coordination value beyond private schedule control.
