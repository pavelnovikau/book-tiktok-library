V–VIII retain committee, Ballinger, Roxton’s past, named guides and roles, secrecy, mutual irritations, guides’ navigation, forest and disputed sighting; travel statistics and much plant taxonomy are omitted. Repeated landscape inventory is compressed. No invented perspective of captives or enemy motives. Specimen procedures and several minor observations are omitted.
