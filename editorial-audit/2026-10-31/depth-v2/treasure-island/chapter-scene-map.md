## Routes

Derived counts including framing: {'essential': 28, 'core': 48, 'deep': 96}.

Essential omits most preparations, detailed stockade evacuation, Tom’s murder and Hands’s introduction, but keeps the chest pursuit, map, misleading Silver encounter, barrel discovery, shore departure, Ben, reunion, negotiations and battle. No later ship/hunt/return outcome appears.

Core retains every draft paragraph through XXI, including Livesey’s separate account; it stops at the same unresolved point with fuller relationships and material action. Deep adds the continuous remaining arc.

The body does not treat Silver’s tales or the doctor’s historical medical beliefs as independently validated science. Allardyce is a guess, Jim’s larger captive claim is a boast, and Silver’s future remains unknown. Both prior Ben findings remain closed in the new telling.
