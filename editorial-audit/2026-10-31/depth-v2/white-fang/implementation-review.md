# Implementation self-review — White Fang depth-v2

This is an implementation handoff, not root acceptance. Only this newly created candidate directory was written. No collector, historical review/ready file, public reader, catalogue, shared skill, global ledger, Git/PR or deployment was changed.

The supplied exact English witness was read completely, in 23 consecutive bounded ranges (front matter included in the first range but excluded from literary-reading credit). All five parts and 25 complete chapters support the connected telling. The old reports were used for orientation and the known lynx correction, not as a substitute for this reading. The old 96-screen effective corpus was sampled for orientation, not certified or reused. The accepted fiction pilot’s personal review, map, connected scenes and build/QA code were consulted; its acceptance is not transferred to this candidate.

Connected original Russian prose came first, corresponding English prose second, then whole-paragraph segmentation. The 85 matching narrative paragraphs cover pursuit → birth and first exploration → camp and separation → sled-work and famine → Fort Yukon and sale → fights and rescue → gradual attachment → southern household → actual ending. Scene groups usually develop over several successive paragraphs. Henry’s embodied fear, One Eye’s snare/porcupine encounters, the cub’s clumsy reversals, the changing relationship with Kiche, the sale/escapes, Cherokee’s grip, Scott and Matt’s disagreement, cautious touch, absence/return, southern humour and the difficult final recovery remain events and relationships rather than headings about adaptation. The final image is allowed to end the narrative without a moral or reader exercise.

The final result has 8,123 RU / 10,009 EN narrative words. That is about45 /45.5 minutes at180/220wpm. It falls within this book’s planning range without using the pilot counts as a quota. The map lists real omissions and compressed exposition chapter by chapter; neither length nor structural QA is offered as editorial acceptance.

The implementation review considered both complete drafts and the order/transitions of nested routes. Deterministic checks prove exact paragraph and embedded-data parity, not literary fullness. Root still must personally read all final RU/EN prose, framing and routes, compare coverage/omissions, check key primary passages and decide whether this is engaging and faithful enough.
