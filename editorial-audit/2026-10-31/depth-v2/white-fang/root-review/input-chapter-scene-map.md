# Chapter and scene map — White Fang

Working review document: contains full-story spoilers. All five parts and 25 complete source chapters were read. Exact witness SHA-256: `3c7226d00fd9b169f0016635d0e461501620f343220ee18db7ca9f083f4ad3f4`. Locators are electronic lines, never print pages.

The connected Russian draft was completed before the English draft; segmentation then retained 85 whole narrative paragraphs, one per reading unit. Final count is 32 / 50 / 88 including cover/final (and one Deep warning). Deep has 8,123 RU narrative words, about45 minutes at180wpm. The length follows the preserved events and relationships below, not a screen or word quota. It gives repeated actions and reactions room—Henry’s siege, the first outing, the sale, Cherokee, trust, the southern household and recovery—while compressing repetitive exposition and incidental speech. It is an original condensation, not the unabridged novel.

Essential’s 30 narrative units follow pursuit, cubhood, camp, separation, chosen return, sled-work, Fort Yukon, sale and fighting to Cherokee’s appearance. Core’s 48 add the longer fire siege, full first outing, snare/porcupine sequences, camp retaliation and later famine, with the same unresolved stopping point. Both advertise that limit honestly and withhold the fight’s result. Deep preserves every selected scene in order and continues through all later decisive events to the puppies. Neither the cover nor shared final states those outcomes.
