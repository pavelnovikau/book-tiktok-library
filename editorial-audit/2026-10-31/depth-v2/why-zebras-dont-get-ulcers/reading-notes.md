# Private-source reading notes (original paraphrase)

Reading ledger records exact displayed/acknowledged source ranges. These notes are orientation for the new condensation, not copied book text or root acceptance.

1. Opening insomnia → imaginative anticipation. Three stressor classes, not humans-only stress; adaptive mobilization, suppressed long investments, allostasis changes set points/coordinates body/anticipates. Selye ovarian extract versus saline control; both mishandled groups affected; temperature/exercise/surgery extensions. His exhaustion explanation rejected: costs of persistent response, poor shutoff, mismatched recovery. Risk through intermediate vulnerabilities, not deterministic illness. Addison and autonomic failure show absence of response dangerous. Zebra title deliberately qualified in note1 (captivity/transport exceptions). Historical Western-disease rhetoric will not become a current claim about conquered infections.
2. Autonomic nerves vs circulating hormones; sympathetic catecholamines fast, CRH → pituitary ACTH → adrenal glucocorticoids slower. Hypothalamic control demonstrated by isolated pituitary losing regulated pattern; releasing/inhibitory signals, Guillemin/Schally tissue purification. Cortisol effects include permissive/preparatory/recovery roles, not only onset. Hormonal fingerprints vary by stressor, context, tissue sensitivity, species. Tend/befriend hypothesis and both-sex counterexamples; no binary universal. Growth hormone species difference.
3. Cardiac rate/force, redistribution, venous return, vasopressin/fluid retention; readiness can differ from active escape. Vessel remodeling → resistance feedback, ventricular hypertrophy, inflammation/atherosclerosis. Kaplan social reorganization: unstable top contenders and stable low-ranking animals; beta blockade intervention and diet interaction. Existing ischemia makes new arousal worse; silent ischemia, arrhythmia versus infarction distinctions. Parasympathetic recovery/variability. Joy can also mobilize diseased heart. Endogenous estrogen/animal results vs WHI2002 hormone trial; book-era unresolved formulations/timing, no hormone recommendation. Voodoo-death accounts have poisoning/starvation/reputational confounds; autonomic explanation is a hypothesis, young deaths unresolved. CRP supremacy/egg rhetoric and historical mortality statistics deliberately not carried forward as clinical advice.
4. Insulin stores amino acids/glucose/fat in protein/glycogen/triglyceride; stress reverses storage, liver gluconeogenesis, active muscles can use mobilized fuel while storage/nonworking tissues resist insulin. Repeated storing/mobilizing costs energy; extreme glucocorticoid exposure muscle wasting. Type1 autoimmune insulin deficiency vs type2 resistance/compensatory secretion; stress can worsen control or unmask existing disease, not proven origin of autoimmunity. Beta-cell decline in type2 must not be mistranslated as becoming autoimmune type1. Seeman over70 cohort: combined subthreshold biomarkers predictive; allostatic load is multisystem accumulation, not one magic hormone. Outdated prevalence figures and simplified adipocyte-count claim omitted.
5. Appetite timing: rapid CRH suppresses; glucocorticoids delayed/longer-lived promote recovery appetite. Continuous severe stress differs from intermittent daily surges; Epel high cortisol reactors eat more/sweets after stress, not necessarily at baseline. Restrained eating also moderates. Visceral receptors and insulin context; Dallman comfort-food feedback can reduce HPA without healthy overall consequence. Gut upper motility/flow vs colon activation; IBS motility/visceral sensitivity, symptom-stress timeseries, recall problems/subtypes. Ulcers: Warren/Marshall infection discovery does not specify why all infected do not ulcerate. Acid rebound after defenses decline, ischemia/reperfusion, immune restraint, prostaglandin repair and uncertain rhythmic contraction; several pathways, not excess acid only. Historical infection percentages not retained as current estimates; NSAIDs/H pylori qualification needed.
6. Growth expensive; GH/somatomedins/thyroid/insulin/sex hormones distinct. Prenatal Dutch hunger winter, trimester/mismatch with later abundant food; Barker small birth size as proxy not precise diagnosis. Maternal glucocorticoid exposure models, feedback receptor reduction; rat amygdala/hippocampus later outcomes; human prenatal/postnatal confounds. Intergenerational maternal physiology explanation does not equal DNA inheritance. Rare profound psychosocial growth failure, not school move/divorce. Nurse case GH5.9→13→6.9→15; growth0.5→1.7→0.6→1.5cm/20d with same food during holiday, case not universal trial. Rat separation/sedated mother/active simulated licking contrast supports active touch. Field premature infant tactile trial (15min 3/day) reported growth/discharge differences, not home treatment instructions. Human acute GH rise mobilizes energy while somatomedin effects blocked; rat immediate GH fall; initiation-ritual correlations have genetic/nutrition/survivorship alternatives and no hormone measures, can omit. Bones ongoing formation/resorption; strong evidence at Cushing/pharmacologic exposure, monkey social stress extension cautious. Harlow cloth vs milk surrogate preference/ethics: attachment not calories alone. Early effects not destiny; later environment can alter trajectory; author explicitly rejects panic over normal imperfect parenting.
7. Reproductive suppression at hypothalamic releasing signal, pituitary sensitivity, gonadal response; opioids/prolactin/glucocorticoids. Autonomic erectile coordination distinct from testosterone; anticipatory performance loop, no diagnostic shortcut. Seasonal mating stress and hyena submission erection exceptions: behavior-specific rewiring hypothesis not proof. Energy availability/cycle; lactational prolactin example Kalahari feeding pattern distinguishes pulses from sustained signal (not contraception advice). Rat lever effort for access to mate operationalizes desire; human relationship context stronger than rodent hormone model. IVF reverse causality/prior failures confound distress-outcome links. Bruce odor effect rodent implantation not human miscarriage explanation. Pregnancy loss timing not preceding-day anecdote; human basic reproduction remarkably resilient versus delicate libido. Kenyan/Hutterite comparison and camps used in book to challenge fragility, not normalize horrific conditions; menstruation not proof ovulation/fertility.
8. Ader/Cohen conditioned immunosuppression flavor+drug then flavor can affect lupus-prone mouse survival. Innate inflammation vs acquired specificity/memory; cytokine cross-talk. Immune→IL1→HPA feedback limits excessive response. Early acute mobilization, later restraint, prolonged severe suppression; Dhabhar migrating cells at skin mean blood counts alone mislead. Munck clamp-low-glucocorticoid experiment supports inadequate brake, not stronger immunity always better. Distinguish stress→hormone→immune measure→clinical illness and behavioral routes, one comparison does not show all links. Cohen nasal virus same dose/controlled surroundings, prolonged social stresses predict more colds, not randomized human stress. Isolation/SIV primates experimental, humans adherence/health selection. Bereavement Israeli parents no overall mortality increase but less support risk; no inevitable broken-heart death. HIV/latent viruses discussed historically selectively, not present treatment account.
Cancer central: implanted/virus tumor growth in stressed animals and sympathetic/glucocorticoid immune, angiogenesis/fuel mechanisms not spontaneous human onset. Retrospective diagnosis-memory, prebiopsy expectations, lifestyle confounds. Western Electric depression/carcinogen reanalysis. Spiegel random allocation metastatic breast cancer support vs usual care showed survival difference; later2001 trial no survival replication; contamination/support changes plausible explanation, not demonstrated resolution. Distress benefit ≠ survival benefit; cortisol/NK marker changes did not prove survival mediation; adherence route plausible. Siegel critique: moving from possible influence to willpower cure blames sick people/parents; support valuable without cure claim. Historical postscript: poor chronically ill autopsy controls have shrunken thymus; healthy sudden-death infants wrongly called enlarged, prophylactic radiation harmed thyroid. Preserve selection-of-normal argument, not book-era SIDS causation claim. Notes not all read.
9. Nociception/spinal relay and inhibitory interneurons, descending modulation; ongoing injury not necessary for sensitized pain. Beecher soldiers need less morphine than similarly injured civilians (meaning/relief); sensory versus affective dimensions, hot-water/hypnosis imaging. Endogenous opioid receptors, antagonist tests of some stress/placebo analgesia; not all analgesia opioid, not universal placebo cure. Author daughter ear-instrument relief before antibiotics anecdote. Strong urgent injury vs anticipatory anxiety often opposite responses; IBS visceral pain exception. Chronic opioid analgesia wanes, unusual exception to Selye rejection, not permanent invulnerability. Fibromyalgia biological hypotheses explicitly book-era unknown, avoid low-cortisol diagnosis.
10. Opening Coke celebration is author's deliberately fanciful joke, NOT true memoir; do not retell as actual case. Explicit/implicit memory: H.M. can learn procedure without remembering prior sessions. Distributed networks, synaptic potentiation explanatory simplification. Cahill/McGaugh neutral hospital visit vs accident story; emotional segment retained better, propranolol removes selective benefit not all memory. Moderate arousal versus extreme stress; retrieval especially vulnerable. High/low affinity hippocampal receptors, amygdala interaction; equal cortisol in mating need not same outcome. Dendrite atrophy can reverse, neurogenesis debate, fuel shortfall worsens injured-neuron crisis, massive/prolonged glucocorticoid models. Cushing size recovery after correction; PTSD antecedent-small-hippocampus alternative (the main text does not describe a twin design); aging correlation reverse causality through feedback. MRI volume ≠ neuron death. No current prescribing claims from old stroke/spinal injury discussion.
11. Newborn episode: confident parent → night chaos/spilled cranberry juice → child finally asleep but father anticipating next wake. NREM/REM distinct; brain active sleep, memory replay McNaughton rat ensembles, deprivation confound versus sleep function, Hairston reversal learning apparent advantage because prior habit not consolidated. Ordinary sleep loss modest HPA response, not fatal cascade. CRH arousal fragments slow sleep; sleep loss/recovery mutual reinforcement. Born wake expectation6am vs9am hormone rise5 vs8 before actual wake. Cho airline staff5 vs15day recovery, duration/zones controlled but observational, not proof irreversible loss. Unpredictable fragmentation more than total hours. No historical hours/prevalence as current statistic.
12. Aging reduced reserve/max response plus slow shutoff; healthy resting measurements can hide difference, timed vs untimed IQ. Salmon/marsupial rapid postmating decline and adrenal removal extreme species case not human longevity protocol. Rate-of-living fixed heartbeat idea rejected/simplified. Dexamethasone feedback test illustrates controller: high hormone should suppress upstream secretion. Hippocampal dysfunction↔glucocorticoid exposure cascade supported rodents; human applicability unresolved and marked heterogeneous. People/rats with preserved function matter, aging not inevitable catastrophe. Cultural old-age vignette not proof no fear in a whole society.
13. Appraisal not negation of physical response: Selye anesthetized incision counters Mason everything psychological. Weiss same shocks: gnawing block/outlet less ulceration, aggression transfers harm not ethical advice. Familiar support vs strangers; predictability also safety intervals, random feeding same calories HPA rises, parachute habituation. Forecast timing/frequency/severity/precision limits; false control can become guilt in unavoidable disaster. Brady executive-monkey nonrandom fast learners more reactive and protocol context; no proof bosses uniquely ulcer-prone. Job process control vs impossible control of every outcome.
14. Major depression anhedonia/guilt/psychomotor and vegetative features; not ordinary sadness or weakness. Real friend's father recovers walking two hospital loops but invents reconstructed smaller corridor to fit deterioration belief. Monoamine drug action rapid vs weeks symptom change; receptor/autoreceptor opposing hypotheses are historical, not proven simple shortage. Brain circuits/ACC exclusion-game example contextual emotional pain. Genes/adoption/twin shared womb/environment; susceptibility not destiny. Cytokine and thyroid/sex hormone links illustrate multifactorial biology, no personal diagnosis. Stress often precipitates early episodes, recurrent episodes can become less tied to external triggers. Elevated HPA/feedback failure in subset, other profiles; not all depression high cortisol. Cushing/steroid comparison suggests contribution not universal cause. Old antiglucocorticoid/DHEA promise unproven therapeutic future. Freud grief/ambivalence/turned aggression is attributed interpretation hard to test. Seligman/Maier yoked uncontrollable shocks→later failure escape when possible vs same controllable shocks; Hiroto noise/control and unsolvable-rule generalization, not equivalent clinical disease. Stress unifies cognition, environment, biology; compensatory synthesis/recovery varies. 5-HTTLPR/Caspi mechanism historically exciting but cannot present as established contemporary gene test; must qualify with primary later research or omit specific allele while retain gene-environment uncertainty.
