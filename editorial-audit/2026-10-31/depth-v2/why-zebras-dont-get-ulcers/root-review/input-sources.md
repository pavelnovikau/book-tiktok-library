## Edition-sensitive distinctions

The rat-pup result in chapter 6 is **separation → lower GH; a sedated mother's presence does not restore it; active licking-like stimulation does**. Chapter 18 separately discusses early handling and natural maternal care in later-life outcomes. The condensation does not invent a mediation chain from separation through increased care after reunion to healthier aging. Both passages are retained, with their distinct purposes, in `unit-028` and `unit-096`.

The RU chapter-4 wording can suggest that later pancreatic failure converts type 2 into juvenile/type 1 diabetes. `unit-017` keeps the mechanisms separate and labels the NIDDK qualification. Medication need is not used as a new disease identity. Warren is named by surname in both languages, avoiding the RU edition's first-name variance. Marshall's self-infection is described as gastritis, not a demonstrated self-induced ulcer.

The private chapter-10 main text raises antecedent hippocampal vulnerability but does not describe a twin design. The older root's explicit Gilbertson twin-study setup is **not** silently attributed to this edition or claimed as a retained case. `unit-055` keeps the actual causal alternative and volume/recovery evidence. The map reflects that narrower scope.

The changing-shock-count scenario is the author's illustration, not a performed experiment. The opening Coke celebration in the memory chapter is a joke, not a factual memoir; it is not retold as an actual event. The upholsterer episode keeps the delayed recognition rather than the author's imagined instant discovery. No dialogue or print pages are fabricated.
