Primary evidence: [actual source](https://www.keenon.tv/around-the-world-in-one-long-depression-liaquat-ahamed-on-1873-the-making-of-the-global-economy/) — Actual Ahamed answer at 16:51–18:20: silver sales, falling silver prices and contraction of international reserves; 34:24 on monetary mismanagement and prolonged falling prices.

### 1873-SR-02 · egypt · medium

Type: speaker/argument misattribution. Routes: deep.

RU: «Коуэн спрашивает, доказывает ли это корыстный мотив вторжения; Ахамед не принимает такого простого вывода. Связь интересов видна, но намерение человека нельзя восстановить из неё одной.»

EN: “Cowen asks whether this proves a profit motive for invasion; Ahamed rejects that simple inference. Interests overlap, but an intention cannot be reconstructed from that overlap alone.”

The recorded exchange has the opposite allocation of the challenge: Ahamed raises the conflict-of-interest question; Cowen proposes a principle-driven repayment decision combined with insider advantage; Ahamed agrees Gladstone did not invade to profit on the bonds. The candidate invents Cowen’s question and turns the actual concession into a generic methodological ending. Bond ownership itself is supported.

Smallest correction (RU): Заменить эти две фразы: «Ахамед ставит вопрос о личной выгоде и убеждённости в обязательности выплаты долгов. Коуэн предлагает второе объяснение, а Ахамед соглашается: по его мнению, Гладстон не вторгался в Египет ради прибыли от своих облигаций».

Smallest correction (EN): Replace these two sentences: “Ahamed raises the question of personal advantage versus a belief that debts must be repaid. Cowen proposes the latter explanation, and Ahamed agrees: in his view, Gladstone did not invade Egypt to profit from his bond holdings.”

Primary evidence: [actual source](https://conversationswithtyler.com/episodes/liaquat-ahamed/) — Gladstone/Egypt exchange: Ahamed’s question after the repayment comparison, Cowen’s Could it be Gladstone thought…, then Ahamed’s Yes, probably… concession (web transcript lines 94–100).

Reported interpretation and explicit concession in the author interview, not proof of Gladstone’s private motive. Do not reinstate the suspect oral debt figures.
