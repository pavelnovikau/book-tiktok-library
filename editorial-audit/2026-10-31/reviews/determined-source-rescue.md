## Findings


### DET-SR-01 — medium — localized factual/experimental precision: reported intention time confused with time of reporting

Stable IDs: libet. Affected routes: core, deep.

Exact RU: В опытах Либета участник делает простое движение и сообщает, когда почувствовал намерение. Регистрируемый мозговой сигнал готовности появляется раньше этого сообщения.

Exact EN: In Libet’s experiments, a participant makes a simple movement and reports when an intention was felt. A recorded readiness signal appears earlier.

The critical comparison is readiness-potential onset versus the moment at which the participant says the intention first arose, not versus the later act of communicating the report. RU explicitly says before this report; that changes the experimental observation into a trivial chronological fact. EN may be read correctly but leaves the comparator implicit. The following sentence about subjective onset helps, yet does not repair the explicit RU comparator. This is a finding about the described experiment, not a current-science efficacy claim or an objection to the author’s thesis.

Minimal RU correction: Заменить второе предложение: «Регистрируемый мозговой сигнал готовности появляется раньше названного участником момента возникновения намерения». Сохранить последующую оговорку: один этот результат не решает спор.

Minimal EN correction: Make the second sentence explicit for parity: “The recorded readiness signal begins before the time the participant reports first feeling the intention.” Retain the following qualification that this result alone does not settle the dispute.

Primary evidence: [printed pp. 22–23 (PDF pp. 35–36), original Libet setup and timing; limits on pp. 28–32 and 39](https://cdn.penguin.co.uk/dam-assets/books/9781529920062/9781529920062-sample.pdf).
