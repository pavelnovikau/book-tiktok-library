## Meaning and reading experience

The opening explains why trying to solve a feeling can keep it active; it then makes the change of attention intelligible before presenting the weekly progression. Lucy's inflexible study/work strategy and Alex's pajamas supply actual human examples. The sequence does not become a productivity framework. Core's added practices clarify how the change is learned; Deep's seat, queue and minute-long return remain connected to it. Essential is a useful overview rather than an eight-week course compressed into instructions.

The important distinctions survive: doing mode remains useful; acceptance is not resignation; a thought need not be a verdict; a scan does not require relaxation; difficulty is manageable and prepared by earlier practices; kindness is not a demand to manufacture warmth. The activity list and three post-pause options belong to the authors and should stay. The ending returns to feasible personal practice rather than a digest-invented formula. RU and EN preserve the same argument and qualifications.
