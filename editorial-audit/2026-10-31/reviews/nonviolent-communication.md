Findings

NVC-01 — two-punishment-questions — medium / P2, blocks PASS.
Affected: Russian Core and Deep.

Exact RU: «Разбирая наказание, Розенберг предлагает два вопроса: что мы хотим, чтобы человек сделал, и почему хотим, чтобы он это сделал?»

Exact EN comparator, which is correct: “Rosenberg asks two questions about punishment: what do we want the person to do, and what do we want their reason for doing it to be?”

The Russian second question asks about the speaker's reason for wanting the act. The author's distinction concerns the other person's motivation for acting. This changes the procedure and obscures the next sentence's argument about coercion. The title alone does not repair that meaning.

Smallest correction: replace only «и почему хотим, чтобы он это сделал?» with «и чем мы хотим, чтобы он при этом руководствовался?» Keep EN.

Evidence: [PuddleDancer's Rosenberg collection](https://nonviolentcommunication.com/resources/mbr-quotes/), entry beginning “If we ask two questions,” second question: “What do we want the other person’s reasons to be for doing as we request?” Web text line 158; the leading 194 is a character count, not a page reference.
