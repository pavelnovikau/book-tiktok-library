**Story and continuity.** This is a spare but readable story. The skipped turn, first sled experience, question after supper, changed game, baby care, recording and forced departure carry the telling. The Giver’s apology and grandfather relationship keep the emotional cost visible. Lowry’s conflict over choice, pain and care remains in the characters’ experiences and decisions; it is not converted into a management lesson, exercise or universal political prescription. Training/war are very compressed, but the account is connected rather than a chapter-administration list.

Essential includes the family-memory antecedent for the love question and ends at the offered recording. Core adds the apple/colour connection, permission to lie, choice and changed friendship without showing the decisive event. Deep supplies the additional memory mechanisms and action needed for the escape sequence. The plan’s expected effect on the community is not narrated as a later confirmed result. The departure has an immediate, established reason; the Giver’s staying has an established reason. No further required continuity defect was found.
