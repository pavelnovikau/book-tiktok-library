All four required findings affect both languages; I found no separate substantive RU/EN contradiction. The omission that matters for this retained arc is specific: Estraven's political explanation, documented in LHD-SR-004, is needed to resolve the mistrust established at supper. Restoring it requires a brief attributed conversation beat, not a new managerial conclusion or an exhaustive political appendix.

Kemmer is presented as fictional worldbuilding; the text does not claim experimental proof about real human biology, derive universal peace from sex or establish an origin theory. The relationship remains more than a diplomatic lesson. The uncertainty in `kemmer-in-the-tent`, `a-dead-brothers-voice` and `the-border` should remain: unexplained experience is not resurrection, an unconsummated relationship is not declared a mistake, and the final act is not certified as suicide or necessary sacrifice.

Closing chronology is sound at the retained resolution: preparations for transmission in Sassinoth precede betrayal/flight; the border episode precedes confinement/recovery, the second audience, crew arrival and the Estre visit. Karhide's willingness is not made unanimous planetary consent. The public accusation is not magically revoked. The family coda remains a request for a story, not a guaranteed reconciliation.
