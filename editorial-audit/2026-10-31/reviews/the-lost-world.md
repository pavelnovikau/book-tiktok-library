## Required finding LW-REQ-01 — `empty-camp`

**Both languages; all routes.** RU: «Ограда развалена … Он зовёт их и стреляет, но ответа нет». EN: “The hedge is wrecked … He calls and fires, but no answer comes.”

This changes the scene’s evidence and invents an action. In XII the enclosure is as Malone left it; its **gate is open**. He shouts without a reply. The lone shot was heard on the way home, before he arrived; he later finds **Roxton’s** empty cartridge. Roxton explains the shot during the capture account in XIII. The mystery should not acquire another shooter or a smashed hedge.

Primary: [Doyle, chapter XII and the capture account in XIII](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/139/pg139-images.html). Local `group-1-private/the-lost-world/primary.txt`, XII lines 5589–5645 (anchors: “And suddenly there came something…”, “The zareba rose before me, even as I had left it, but the gate was open”, “Lord John’s … empty cartridge”); XIII lines 5798–5810 (“I shot one of them through the belly”).

Smallest correction: change the wrecked hedge to an open gate and remove Malone’s firing. For example, RU «Вход в ограду открыт, вещи разбросаны, товарищи исчезли. Он зовёт их, но ответа нет»; EN “The gate stands open, belongings are scattered, his companions are gone. He calls, but no answer comes.” No additional scene, screen or explanation is required. “Wrecked camp” in the neutral final can still describe scattered belongings; it need not be rewritten.
