## Required TI-REQ-01 — `skeleton-pointer`

RU: «Люди цепенеют, пока Сильвер не убеждает их, что это живой Бен Ганн». EN: “The men freeze until Silver persuades them it is the living Ben Gunn.”

This replaces Stevenson’s particular comic cause with a different explanation. Silver argues from an echo, then recognises Ben’s voice. Dick explicitly objects that Ben is no more present in the body than Flint. The older pirates recover because **nobody minds Ben, dead or alive**. They do not all accept a proof that Ben is living. That joke explains their returned courage.

Primary: [chapter XXXII](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/120/120-h/120-h.htm), private `group-1-private/treasure-island/primary.txt`, lines **6985–7018**. Anchors: “There was an echo”, “Ben Gunn’s not here in the body any more’n Flint”, “dead or alive, nobody minds him”.

Smallest correction: remove the assertion that Silver convinces them Ben is alive; say he recognises Ben’s voice and they fear Ben neither alive nor dead, so continue. RU example: «Сильвер узнаёт голос Бена Ганна. Бена они не боятся ни живым, ни мёртвым, и идут дальше». EN: “Silver recognises Ben Gunn’s voice. Alive or dead, nobody fears Ben, and they continue.” This restores a source detail and causal link, not a preferred prose style.

## Required TI-REQ-02 — `silver-slips-away`

RU: «при помощи Бена, который боялся его мести». EN: “with a bag of coins and Ben’s help: Ben feared his revenge”.

**Low severity, one-word specificity problem.** Ben’s fear is established; the particular revenge motive is not. The primary narrator says Ben was terribly afraid of his old quartermaster. After the escape, Ben **claims** he assisted it to preserve their lives if Silver stayed aboard. The retelling turns that into narrator-certified fear of revenge.

Primary: [chapter XXXIV](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/120/120-h/120-h.htm), local lines **7373–7382** and **7465–7473**. Smallest correction: «…при помощи Бена, который боялся Сильвера» / “…with Ben’s help; Ben was afraid of Silver.” Alternatively keep Ben’s own explanation with attribution. No added dialogue, motive or future fate is needed.
