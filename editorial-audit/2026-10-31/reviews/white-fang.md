## Reader judgment

This is a connected condensed novel. The ominous sled pursuit leads to the she-wolf, den and cub; camp takes his mother and denies play; his own violence, the sale and cage lead into the very different struggle with Cherokee. The rescue does not produce instant gratitude. The bandaged hand, meat and feared touch make trust gradual, and the head-hidden gesture gives it a concrete emotional expression. The broken window makes the southern journey a consequential choice. The southern home requires another adjustment before the final attack and quiet puppy scene.

The animal is not made an innocent mascot: persecution, theft and killing remain. Scott is not rewritten as wholly nonviolent: his blows during chicken training remain. Hall is not reduced to a generic criminal villain or the judge turned into a knowing conspirator: the wrongful last conviction and unequal knowledge survive. The last image is allowed to remain an image instead of becoming an exercise or lesson. Londons animal experience and reflections remain literary narration; they do not need intrusive zoological warnings.

Essential resolves Henrys immediate danger explicitly, follows the cub through the sale, and stops at the new opponent. Core adds the feeding mystery, fire siege, forest reversals, lynx, return to camp and famine. Both stops are coherent under their stated spoiler promise. Deep contains the whole represented later arc after the warning. Both languages carry the same causal and emotional content.
