## Concrete guide hazards excluded

- SparkNotes' compressed final section reverses official wife/concubine language for Irulan and Chani. The rebuilt `the-settlement` instead describes a political marriage with Irulan and Paul's personal promise to Chani, checked against the more detailed final recap. No invented legal outcome or sequel event.
- SparkNotes' descriptions of Mentats as emotionless, Paul as wholly controlling events, and political victory as guaranteed peace are not adopted. Primary quoted vision passages explicitly preserve contingency, blind spots and the threat of jihad.
- Some guide precision around the Water of Life and chronology of its administration is not reliable enough to dramatize a detailed chemical/medical procedure. The retained `pauls-ordeal` says Paul takes it, falls into a trance, Chani is summoned and recognizes the cause, and he awakens. It does not copy the guide's instantaneous-waking explanation or prescribe an intervention.
- A guide's approximate far-future date, dubious serial-magazine assertion, speculative authorial intention and commentary about religions/gender/real science are excluded. The edition boundary is the 1965 first novel, its three internal books; later Dune books and film chronology are excluded.
