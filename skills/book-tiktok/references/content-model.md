# Content model

Use when researching, writing, translating or checking fidelity. Start with the actual work and edition, not a universal digest template.

## Choose the book’s form

- **Fiction narrative:** tell the author’s story in engaging original prose. Preserve characters, relationships, motives established in the text, events, causality, atmosphere, humour, uncertainty and suspense. Scenes and concrete events carry the reading. Titles can name a place, scene, action or question. Do not turn adventures into lessons about management, leadership, competence, psychology or the reader’s life. No invented scenes, dialogue, motives or authorial intentions.
- **Explanatory nonfiction:** convey the author’s main ideas, reasoning and real examples in a connected sequence. Preserve attribution, qualifications, disagreement and uncertainty. Do not substitute the digest writer’s analogies, prescriptions or lessons for the book. Independent factual checks qualify claims explicitly rather than replacing the author’s argument.
- **Practical or hybrid works:** retain the book’s actual procedures and limits; preserve a philosophical novel’s narrative and argument in their original relationship. Choose narrative or explanatory presentation according to the text being written. An original research brief must be labeled as such, not passed off as an authored book.

No form requires a management takeaway, moral, mental model or motivational synthesis. A book’s real philosophical or practical argument remains part of that book; the digest writer must not add one by formula.

## Evidence and source ledger

Prefer the user-supplied owned edition or a lawful public-domain/licensed text. Official author/publisher excerpts and interviews, primary factual sources and substantial independent reviews can help verify or qualify it. Tertiary summaries are discovery aids, not a substitute for the primary work.

Check the exact edition, abridgment status and identity of local EPUBs. A fetched full text, consultation of selected passages and continuous reading are different evidence scopes. Record only the work actually done. Never seek pirated copies or include private/full copyrighted text in a public repository.

Keep a working ledger of direct URL or private edition locator, title, author/publisher, role, access date, consulted sections, what each supports/challenges and remaining uncertainties. Paraphrase useful evidence; do not copy chapters. Reviews and blurbs cannot certify unsupported scenes or an entire book. If evidence is inadequate, report a precise source gap instead of filling it with plausible inventions.

Before prose, map the book in its own form: the sequence of characters/events and causal links for fiction; the thesis, reasoning, mechanisms, cases and qualifications for nonfiction; actual steps and conditions for practice. Cross-check concrete scenes and mechanisms against actual source passages. Preserve unresolved questions and competing interpretations.

## Prose first, screens second

Write a coherent, engaging account in original words before segmentation. Read it continuously, then divide at useful narrative or explanatory beats. Let body length follow the beat and phone readability; word-count bands are diagnostic, never quotas. Merge repetitive abstractions. Do not pad, mechanically split paragraphs or invent examples to retain historical totals.

Use nested Essential ⊂ Core ⊂ Deep. Essential must work as an intelligible story or argument in its own right; Core and Deep insert meaningful detail without breaking the sequence. When endings are reserved for Deep, the shallower story may stop at a coherent unresolved point rather than promise a complete ending. The shared level-1 final screen must also respect that boundary.

Honor explicit user length constraints. When the task leaves length to you, choose it without another confirmation gate, then derive exact cumulative `targetModes` from the justified finished screens, cover and final included. Counts are structural assertions, not evidence of quality. Explain any conflict with an explicit numerical brief before changing that constraint.

Check each mode’s continuity and spoiler promise. Keep decisive fictional endings behind the agreed Deep warning/consent; do not leak them through Essential/Core, covers, contents visible before consent, resume labels, shared final copy, or global Sources/method notes.

## Provenance and reading presentation

Retain screen source maps in schema v2:

- `book` — the author’s narrative, argument, example, procedure or qualification;
- `corroboration` — independent evidence supporting, narrowing or disputing a factual claim;
- `synthesis` — a clearly attributed digest inference, only when useful and permitted by the brief.

There is no required proportion of these types. `book` does not mean a quotation or uncontested truth. In narrative presentation (`contentMode: "narrative"`), omit per-screen academic labels and source-footnote chrome. Preserve the embedded source maps and accessible global Sources dialog. `contentMode: "explanatory"` retains the provenance interface. An absent field preserves legacy behavior. Changing a label never establishes editorial repair.

Sources should describe their actual role and access boundary, in spoiler-safe bilingual notes. The method note must describe the actual work: original-word retelling or condensed explanation, consulted evidence, gaps and any independent qualification. Never claim continuous/full reading from a download or a handful of passages.

## RU/EN review

Keep stable IDs, levels, provenance, source IDs and media shared. Translate meaning and cadence, preserving names, quantities, causes, uncertainty and spoiler boundaries. Localize all interface text, accessibility labels, captions and method/source notes. A language switch preserves position, depth, theme and saved progress.

Read every RU/EN screen in every resulting mode. Compare the effective embedded corpus in the final HTML to the canonical JSON; old local scripts or patches can cause divergence. A lexical scan, source count or 68-pass structure audit does not establish editorial fidelity.
